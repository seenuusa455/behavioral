1. Introduction / Background

These are almost always asked.

Tell me about yourself
Walk me through your resume
Tell me about your current role
Why software engineering?
What kind of work do you enjoy most?
What motivates you?
What are you most proud of in your career?
2. Project Deep Dive (VERY IMPORTANT)

Google especially spends a lot of time here.

Tell me about a project you worked on end-to-end
Describe the most technically challenging project
Tell me about a large-scale distributed system you designed
What was your biggest impact project?
Tell me about a migration/modernization effort
Describe a project where you improved performance/scalability
Tell me about a production issue you solved
Describe a system you optimized significantly
What tradeoffs did you make?
What would you do differently now?

For you, strong examples:

AWS Transfer Family migration
Kafka/EFS optimization
Secure Transport replacement
Large-scale PostgreSQL indexing optimization
Rust performance tuning
Production incident handling
3. Conflict / Disagreement Questions

These are extremely common.

Tell me about a disagreement with a teammate
Tell me about a disagreement with your manager
Describe a situation where stakeholders disagreed
Tell me about a time you pushed back
How do you handle difficult team members?
Describe a time you had to influence without authority
Tell me about a decision you strongly disagreed with
How do you handle criticism?

Google interviewers especially look for:

Humility
Collaboration
Data-driven thinking
Non-defensive communication
4. Leadership & Ownership

Critical for L5/L6.

Tell me about a time you led a project
Describe leadership without formal authority
Tell me about a time you took ownership
Describe a critical production incident you handled
Tell me about a time you went above expectations
Describe mentoring a junior engineer
Tell me about driving alignment across teams
Describe handling ambiguity in leadership
5. Failure / Mistakes

Very common at Google and Amazon.

Tell me about a failure
Tell me about a mistake you made
Describe a project that didn’t go well
Tell me about a missed deadline
What’s your biggest professional regret?
Tell me about a bad technical decision
Describe a time your design failed in production

Important:

Don’t blame others
Show ownership
Explain lessons learned
Explain prevention mechanisms added later
6. Handling Pressure / Constraints

You already identified this category correctly.

Tell me about a high-pressure situation
Describe handling tight deadlines
Tell me about competing priorities
Describe working with limited resources
Tell me about handling production outages
How do you prioritize work?
Describe balancing speed vs quality
Tell me about a time requirements changed suddenly
7. Technical Fluency / Decision Making

This becomes important for senior roles.

Explain a difficult technical decision
Tell me about a tradeoff you made
Describe scaling challenges
Why did you choose a specific technology?
Tell me about improving system reliability
Explain a design decision you defended
Describe reducing latency or improving throughput
Tell me about debugging a hard issue
8. Behavioral “Googliness” Questions

Very important for Google.

Tell me about learning something quickly
Describe working in ambiguity
Tell me about adapting to change
Tell me about collaborating cross-functionally
Describe helping a struggling teammate
Tell me about receiving difficult feedback
Tell me about making an inclusive decision
Describe a time you changed your mind
9. Career / Motivation Questions

You already captured many of these.

Why do you want to work here?
Why are you leaving your current company?
What are you looking for next?
Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
What kind of culture do you prefer?
Why this team/domain?
What excites you technically?

Avoid:

Salary-only answers
Complaining about current employer
Generic company praise
10. Weakness / Self-Awareness

Almost always appears in some form.

What is your biggest weakness?
What skill are you improving?
Tell me about feedback you received
What would your manager say you need to improve?
What’s an area you struggle with?

Good answers:

Real but manageable weakness
Clear improvement effort
Demonstrated progress
11. Team Collaboration

Especially important at large companies.

Describe working with QA/Product/Business teams
Tell me about cross-team dependencies
Describe resolving communication gaps
Tell me about difficult stakeholders
Describe handling conflicting priorities across teams
Tell me about coordinating a release

Your UKHSA/client coordination examples are very useful here.

12. Behavioral Questions Specific to Senior Engineers

These differentiate L5/L6.

Tell me about influencing architecture direction
Describe scaling an organization/process
Tell me about long-term technical vision
Describe balancing technical debt vs feature delivery
Tell me about creating operational excellence
Describe improving engineering productivity
Tell me about preventing future incidents
Explain how you drove standardization
